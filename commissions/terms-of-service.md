# Terms of Service — ToDy Commissions

_Last updated: November 2026_

By commissioning me, you agree to these terms.

## 1. General
- I have the right to decline any commission without giving a reason.
- I currently work in **greyscale and sketch** styles. Please check my portfolio before ordering — I draw in my own style.
- Communication happens through DMs, VGen, or email. Please be patient: I reply within 48 hours.

## 2. What I draw
**✅ Will draw:** OCs, humans, humanoids, D&D/TTRPG characters, fan art, light gore.
**❌ Won't draw:** NSFW/fetish content, real people, hate symbols or hateful content, mecha, highly complex backgrounds.
Not sure? Ask me first!

## 3. Payment
- All prices are in **USD**. Payment via **PayPal invoice** only (or through VGen / Ko-fi if you ordered there).
- Orders **under $40** — full payment upfront.
- Orders **$40 and over** — 50% upfront, 50% after the sketch is approved.
- I start working only after payment is received.
- Payment must be completed within **3 days** of receiving the invoice, otherwise your slot goes to the next person.

## 4. Process
1. You send the order form with references.
2. I confirm and send the invoice.
3. **Sketch stage** — I send a rough sketch for your approval.
4. **Final stage** — I finish the artwork and send the final file.

## 5. Revisions
- **Sketch stage:** up to **2 free** revisions (pose, composition, details).
- **Final stage:** small fixes only (colour of an item, small details). Big changes (new pose, new outfit) = extra fee.
- Changes not caused by my mistake after the sketch is approved: **+$5–15** depending on size.

## 6. Turnaround
- Usual time: **1–3 weeks** from payment.
- Rush (under 5 days): **+30%**, if I have the time.
- I'll keep you updated. If I'm delayed, I'll let you know.

## 7. Refunds
- **Before the sketch is started:** full refund.
- **After the sketch is sent:** 50% refund.
- **After the final artwork is sent:** no refund.
- If I can't finish your commission for any reason, you get a full refund for unfinished work.
- Opening a PayPal chargeback/dispute without contacting me first = permanent ban from commissioning me.

## 8. Usage rights
- **Personal use (allowed):** avatars, banners, printing for yourself, posting with credit, using as a reference for your OC, VTT tokens.
- **Please credit me** when posting: `art by @_ToDyWhatever` (or link my profile).
- **Not allowed without a commercial license:** selling prints, merch, NFTs, using in paid products, books, games, or ads. Commercial use = **price ×2**, discuss with me first.
- **Not allowed at all:** training AI models on my art, feeding it into AI tools, claiming the art as your own, removing my signature.
- I keep the copyright and may post the artwork in my portfolio and social media. If you need it private, tell me before ordering (+20%).

## 9. Files
- You'll get a high-resolution PNG (min. 2000 px on the long side).
- Delivery via DM / email / Google Drive link.
- Keep your files — I don't guarantee storing them after 3 months.
